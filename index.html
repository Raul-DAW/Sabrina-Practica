Biography

Sabrina Carpenter grew up in a small town in Pennsylvania with her parents and her three older sisters, Sarah, Shannon, and Cayla. She had a happy childhood surrounded by her family and nature. As a child, she was very creative, funny, and full of energy. She enjoyed dancing and spending time with her sisters, who were very important to her.

As a young girl, Sabrina was inspired by famous artists like Christina Aguilera and Adele. She loved listening to different types of music and discovering new ways to express herself. Unlike most teenagers, she spent much of her time preparing for auditions and working on her artistic skills. Her passion for performing eventually led her to Los Angeles, where she began exploring opportunities in the entertainment industry. In 2011, she made her first television appearance in Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. This experience was an important step towards her future career and helped her gain confidence in front of the cameras.
